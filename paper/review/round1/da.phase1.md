## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): From an adversarial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the design, data processing, and statistical reporting would survive a hostile attempt to reproduce or reinterpret them. A challenger would press on whether analytic choices were fixed in advance or tuned after seeing results, whether stated uncertainty genuinely reflects the resampling scheme used, and whether enough detail is given that an independent analyst could rebuild the pipeline. I do not score this dimension, but weaknesses here feed directly into whether the central argument has a trustworthy evidential base.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): This dimension tests whether the substantive claims match what the field currently knows, whether earlier work is described fairly rather than selectively, and whether technical terms and reported findings are used correctly. The adversarial angle is to look for convenient misreadings of prior literature, overstated agreement or disagreement with earlier studies, and terminology that quietly shifts meaning to favour the thesis. I do not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by me as Devil's Advocate): This is the dimension I own. It asks whether the core thesis holds together from start to finish, whether each conclusion is actually carried by the evidence presented rather than by assertion, and whether the reasoning contains fallacies that would collapse the main argument if exposed. My job is to build the strongest counter-reading, identify alternative explanations the authors have not ruled out, check that every number is interpreted within the analysis that produced it, and test whether the headline claim still stands once rival explanations and scope limits are taken seriously.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by the perspective seat): This dimension asks whether readers from neighbouring fields could follow the framing and definitions, and whether any claim that crosses disciplinary lines is backed rather than borrowed loosely. The adversarial concern is imported concepts used as rhetorical weight without the supporting evidence from the field they come from. I do not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by the editor-in-chief seat): This dimension covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains itself, the quality of its figures and tables, and conformity with venue conventions. The adversarial concern is presentation that obscures weaknesses, such as key caveats buried far from the claims they qualify or displays that visually overstate a result. I do not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief seat): This dimension asks whether the work suits its intended outlet and adds something original and significant for that readership. The adversarial concern is novelty asserted rather than demonstrated against existing work. No review target has been confirmed for this call, so I make no claim about fit to any particular venue, and I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace the central thesis from question to conclusion; confirm each headline claim is supported by a specific result interpreted within its own analysis and population; list unexcluded rival explanations (confounding, selection, measurement artefact, benchmark construction, reverse causation); check that descriptive or predictive findings are not recast as causal or normative conclusions; check consistency of claims across abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion; check that limitations stated in one place are respected in claims made elsewhere.
what_triggers_block: A principal conclusion depends on an inferential leap the presented evidence cannot carry, such as causal or equity attributions drawn from associational or model-performance results, or a plausible rival explanation that would reverse the headline interpretation is left unaddressed, requiring substantial reanalysis or reframing to fix.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument holds, but some secondary claims are phrased more strongly than their evidence, some numbers are cited outside the context of the analysis that produced them, minor internal inconsistencies appear between sections, or alternative explanations are acknowledged without adequate discussion of how they bear on the conclusions.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis is internally contradicted by the manuscript's own reported results, or rests on a circular or definitional argument such that no revision short of abandoning or replacing the central claim could make the argument coherent.
