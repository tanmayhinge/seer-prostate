## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer): The design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility provisions must reach the level a peer reviewer in this field would expect. From a domain-accuracy seat I do not score this, but I note where a methodological choice changes what a domain claim can legitimately mean, and pass that to the methodology owner.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by me as the domain reviewer): Every substantive claim must be consistent with the current evidence base in the stated field, prior studies must be cited and summarised faithfully, and domain terminology, clinical definitions, benchmarks and reported results must be free of factual error. This is the only dimension I score.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with the methodology reviewer eligible): The central thesis must hang together, the evidence presented must actually carry the conclusions drawn, and no reasoning fallacy may undermine the main argument. I do not score it, but a domain misstatement that props up the thesis is relevant to it and I will flag such overlap rather than score it.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer): Framing, definitions and implications should be understandable to readers from neighbouring fields, and any claim that crosses disciplines should be substantiated. I do not score it; my role is limited to confirming that field-specific definitions are stated correctly so adjacent readers are not misled.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief): Organisation, clarity, figures and tables, and conformity with venue conventions. I do not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief): Whether the work fits the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution for that readership. I do not score it and, because no author-confirmed review target is bound for this call, I make no venue-fit claim of any kind.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether clinical, epidemiological and health-equity statements match current published evidence and guidance; whether cited prior studies are described accurately (direction, magnitude, population, period) and verifiable; whether registry variable definitions, staging and risk-group terms, treatment categories and any external benchmark are named and applied correctly and in their original context; whether each reported number is interpreted in the frame of the analysis that produced it; and whether the claims stay within what the stated data source and cited sources can support.
what_triggers_block: One or more factual errors or misrepresentations of prior work or of an external benchmark that materially alter a headline claim or its interpretation, but which could be corrected without discarding the analysis.
what_triggers_warn: Localised inaccuracies, imprecise or inconsistent domain terminology, outdated or incomplete citation of key evidence, or claims modestly overstated beyond their sources, none of which changes the main conclusions.
what_triggers_fatal: The central finding rests on a fundamental domain error, such as a wrong clinical definition or benchmark underpinning the main outcome, or a fabricated or unverifiable key source, so that the core conclusion cannot stand even after revision.
