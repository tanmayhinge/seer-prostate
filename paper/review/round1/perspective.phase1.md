## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): the design, the handling of the data, the way statistics are reported, and the materials offered for reproduction have to reach the standard a referee in the field would expect. Seen from a cross-disciplinary angle, this matters because readers from neighbouring fields can only reuse or check an analysis when its analytic steps are laid out openly enough to follow without specialist shorthand. I do not score this dimension.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): what the work claims must agree with the present state of evidence in its subject area, earlier studies must be described faithfully, and technical terms and reported results must be free of factual mistakes. From my angle, errors in domain vocabulary would spread when adjacent-field readers borrow the terms, so accuracy is a precondition for sound translation across disciplines. I do not score this dimension.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate seat with methodology also eligible): the central thesis must not contradict itself, the evidence offered must actually carry the claims made, and no reasoning fallacy may weaken the main line of argument. For cross-disciplinary readers, a coherent chain from evidence to conclusion is what allows a finding to be carried into another field without distortion. I do not score this dimension.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by my perspective seat): the framing, the definitions of key constructs, and the stated implications should be understandable to readers who work in adjacent fields, and any claim that crosses disciplinary lines should be backed by evidence or appropriate citation rather than asserted. This is the single dimension I score, and a block here would on its own push the panel toward major revision under the high-priority failure condition.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief seat): the organisation of the document, how clearly ideas are explained, the quality of figures and tables, and conformity with the conventions of the outlet are assessed here. From my angle, clear structure lowers the entry cost for non-specialist readers, but I do not score this dimension.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief seat): the work must suit the configured outlet and offer an original and meaningful contribution for that outlet's audience. Because this call has no author-confirmed target, I make no judgement about fit to any outlet, and I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether core constructs (for example any measure of social position, any benchmark or threshold, any notion of clinical need, and any model-based quantity) are defined operationally in plain terms on first use; whether the framing states which disciplines the work speaks to and why; whether claims that borrow from or speak to health equity, health policy, clinical practice, or machine learning are tied to cited evidence or to the study's own results; whether implications for adjacent fields are stated with their limits, including transportability across health systems; and whether alternative framings from neighbouring disciplines are acknowledged where they would change interpretation.
what_triggers_block: A central construct or benchmark on which the main conclusion depends is undefined or defined in a way an adjacent-field reader cannot reproduce, or an interdisciplinary or cross-system implication that is presented as a principal takeaway rests on no supporting evidence or citation, so that a reader from a neighbouring field would be materially misled about what the findings mean.
what_triggers_warn: Framing and definitions are broadly usable but some secondary terms rely on unexplained field jargon, some cross-disciplinary implications are stated more strongly than the cited support warrants, relevant perspectives from adjacent disciplines are omitted without affecting the main conclusion, or limits on transferring findings to other settings are mentioned only briefly.
